
### Learnings
- powering breadboard with battery
- Ohms law
  -> V (Volts) = I (Current, Amps) x R (resistance, Ohms)
  
     I = V/R
  
     R = V/I
  
- series vs parallel circuit
- series: Divide voltage
- parallel: every device recieves full voltage
